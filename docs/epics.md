# Epics - CultivAR Refinement & AI Readiness

**Parent:** PRD v3.0
**Status:** In Progress

## EPIC-01: Core Journaling Refinement ("The Smoother Experience")
**Description:** Enhance the core daily loops for growers to reduce friction and improve visual feedback.
**Goal:** Reduce "Time to Log" by 30% and increase user satisfaction with visual tracking.
**Related Features:** Visual Timeline, Batch Actions, Gallery.

## EPIC-02: Environmental Intelligence ("The Smart Monitor")
**Description:** Transform raw sensor data into actionable cultivation metrics.
**Goal:** Provide growers with derived metrics (VPD) and proactive safeguards (Alerts).
**Related Features:** VPD Algo, Alert Engine.

## EPIC-03: AI Readiness ("The Brain Uplift")
**Description:** Prepare the platform for LLM integration by exposing context and providing a chat interface.
**Goal:** Enable a "Conversation with your Garden".
**Related Features:** Context API, Chat UI.

## EPIC-GNFT-001: Genetics Marketplace with Fractional G-NFTs ("The Breeder Economy")
**Description:** Comprehensive genetics marketplace enabling breeders to sell seed genetics as fractional NFTs with hybrid minting, state compliance, escrow payments, fraud prevention, and gamification.
**Goal:** Onboard 100+ breeders and list 500+ genetics within 6 months while maintaining 90%+ germination rate and <1% fraud.
**Status:** In Progress (Phase 1.1 Complete - Database Schema)
**Related Features:** G-NFT minting, hybrid minting, compliance system, crypto coins, multi-stage reputation, 15-category leaderboards, secondary market, QR verification.
**Phases:** 10 phases, 56 stories
**Timeline:** 30 weeks (~7 months)
**Details:** See [Epic Document](epic-gnft-001-genetics-marketplace.md)
