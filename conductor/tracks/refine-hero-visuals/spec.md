# Spec: Hero Card Visual Refinement

## Goal
Refine the very first card (Hero card) on the landing page by reversing its gradient direction/colors and ensuring all text is pure white.

## Requirements
- Target: `HeroContent` card in `landing_screen.dart`.
- Gradient: Reverse the colors (Violet to Electric Green).
- Text: Change `colorScheme.onSurface` to `Colors.white` for all text elements in the Hero card.

## Success Criteria
- [ ] Hero card gradient starts with Violet and ends with Electric Green.
- [ ] All text in the Hero card is pure white.
- [ ] Other cards (FeatureSections) remain unchanged.
