# Product Guidelines: CultivAR

## Visual Identity
- **Primary Colors**: Electric Green, Deep Space Black, Neon Purple.
- **Aesthetic**: Premium, Cyberpunk-adjacent, Scientific, High-tech.
- **Components**: Glassmorphism, Rounded Corners (24px default), Smooth Animations.

## Prose Style
- Professional, authoritative, yet innovative and accessible to craft growers.
- Avoid generic marketing fluff; focus on data and results.
