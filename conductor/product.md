# Product: CultivAR

## Users
- Home growers
- Professional breeders
- Commercial cultivation facilities

## Product Goals
- Provide genetic excellence and cultivation intelligence.
- Offer precision analytics and environmental control.
- Enable a decentralized marketplace for genetics and equipment.

## High-level Features
### 🌿 Core Cultivation
- **Plant Tracking**: Full lifecycle management (Clone -> Veg -> Bloom -> Harvest).
- **Strain Management**: Detailed genetic database with terpene and cannabinoid profiles.
- **Journaling**: Activity logging, image uploads, and timeline visualization.

### 🤖 Intelligent Control (MC - Master Controller)
- **Sensor Integration**: Native support for AC Infinity (UIS), Ecowitt, and generic MQTT sensors.
- **Environmental Control**: Dynamic VPD targeting, HVAC automation, and CO2 monitoring.
- **Precision Analytics**: Live telemetry and trend analysis via fl_chart.

### 🧬 Genetics & Trust
- **Genetic Lineage**: Verifiable phenotypic history (Trust Engine).
- **Pheno-Hunting**: Tools for breeding selection and stabilization.
- **Seedbank Integration**: Dynamic seed and clone inventory management.

### 💰 Economy & Community
- **Decentralized Marketplace**: P2P trade of genetics and used equipment.
- **Gamification**: Breeder reputation points (Social feature) and achievement systems.
- **News & Community**: Integrated blog, newsletter, and social feed.
